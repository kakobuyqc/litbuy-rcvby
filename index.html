04 / Litbuy shopping guide

A shortlist that helps
you make a decision.

Use the same comparison method for every listing. Consistent notes make Litbuy finds easier to review and reduce the chance of overlooking a size or cost detail.

01

Save the exact link

Start your Litbuy product links list with the listing URL, seller name, category, and selected color. Record the date checked. Similar pictures can appear on different listings, so save the exact item and variant you reviewed.

02

Measure before choosing

Compare the seller’s measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, note chest width, length, and sleeves. For shoes, check the stated sizing system and any available foot-length guidance.

03

Review before ordering

Reopen the listing to check stock, price, material details, and return terms. Review any available inspection photos against the selected variant. Keep unresolved questions visible until the seller answers them.

05 / A practical spreadsheet structure

What belongs in each row?

These fields form a useful starting point for your own document. This page offers a structure and category links, rather than a downloadable or live inventory spreadsheet.

Suggested columns for organizing fashion product links
Field What to record Why it helps
Product & category A descriptive item name and one primary category Separates sneakers, clothing, headwear, and accessories.
Source link The exact listing URL, seller, and selected variant Lets you return to the same product rather than a similar image.
Size & measurements Size label, sizing system, and relevant measurements Makes fit comparisons more useful than size labels alone.
Materials & photos Declared composition, photo source, and visible details Keeps seller claims separate from what you can actually observe.
Price & currency Item price, currency, and the date of the quote Prevents old or differently denominated prices from being mixed.
Other costs Applicable delivery, service, payment, and import charges Supports a fuller purchase budget.
Review status Shortlisted, awaiting details, checked, or removed Shows which items still need attention.
The photo check

Look past the first image.

A polished cover image is only one piece of information. For a useful product photo comparison, look for views of the front, back, interior, fastenings, and material texture.

  • Match the pictured color and size to your selected variant.
  • Distinguish listing photos from photos of a specific order.
  • Check seams, closures, sole edges, and included components.
  • Record uncertainty when lighting or missing angles limit what you can see.
The budget check

Compare the complete cost.

A low item price is not the same as a low delivered cost. Keep estimated and confirmed amounts in separate columns, and use one currency when comparing alternatives.

Item price + applicable delivery + service or payment fees + applicable import charges

Shipping can depend on the destination, parcel dimensions, weight, and selected service. Check the relevant provider’s current quote and terms before paying.

Common questions about using a Litbuy spreadsheet and browsing the categories linked from this guide.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping-list document used to organize product links, categories, prices, sizes, and comparison notes. A spreadsheet entry is a research starting point; it does not verify a seller or guarantee stock, authenticity, or product quality.

How should I compare product links?

Compare the exact product variant, seller measurements, material description, dated photos, item price, and relevant fees. Save the listing URL and the date checked so that you can revisit changing information.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides an independent shopping guide, a suggested spreadsheet structure, and links to CAFANS fashion categories.

Do spreadsheet links prove that branded goods are authentic?

No. A brand name, spreadsheet entry, product image, or low price does not establish authenticity. Use an authorized retailer when authenticity matters and verify any seller claims independently.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim affiliation with Litbuy or any featured fashion brand.
